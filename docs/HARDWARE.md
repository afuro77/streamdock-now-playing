## Which image shows what?

**Official-store photographs** show the physical product and its standard/vendor
presentation. They do not demonstrate this project's Spotify/Desktop/YouTube
screens. Open them on the product page or via the external image link above.

**`docs/assets/overview.svg` and `overview.ja.svg`** are original explanatory
illustrations of this project's media layout. Their sample artwork is original;
they are not hardware photos, screenshots or evidence of another device working.

**An actual project demo recording** is still to be made. Its recording plan is
in [DEMO_PLAN.md](DEMO_PLAN.md); it must not be replaced by a manufacturer photo
labelled as this software running.

## Image handling

No vendor-photo redistribution license is recorded for this repository. We link
to the source pages and image instead of downloading, altering or committing the
vendor photos. The project's MIT license does not cover those photographs or
vendor trademarks. The external image URL may change; use the product page if it
stops working. No affiliate/referral identifiers are added to these links.
